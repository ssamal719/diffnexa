Why arbitration clauses are everywhere

Published 2 March 2026

Campaigners say arbitration clauses have spread through consumer contracts over the past decade. The regulator has criticised several companies over their approach to refunds, and researchers point to limits on liability as a common complaint.

What the research found

The study examined 1,500 contracts and found that most contained some form of dispute clause. Researchers noted that data protection wording varied widely.

What happens next

A consultation closes in June and the regulator will report later this year.
